# Figma Handoff Checklist

## Required viewports

Create or verify distinct responsive designs for:
- Desktop: 1440 px wide (suggested)
- Tablet: 768–820 px wide (suggested)
- Mobile: 390 px wide (suggested)

Exact dimensions may be adjusted to match the official sprint brief.

## Screens to verify

- [ ] Sign-in
- [ ] Dashboard
- [ ] Appointments list
- [ ] Appointment details
- [ ] Patient details
- [ ] My Appointments

## Responsive behavior to verify

- [ ] Desktop sidebar collapses or transforms appropriately on smaller widths.
- [ ] Tables become readable cards or intentionally scroll without breaking the page.
- [ ] Search, filters, and primary actions remain usable on mobile.
- [ ] Forms have visible labels and useful validation/error states.
- [ ] Text does not clip or overlap at the three target widths.
- [ ] Buttons and controls have comfortable touch targets.
- [ ] Empty, loading, error, and success states are designed.
- [ ] Focus and keyboard navigation are considered.
